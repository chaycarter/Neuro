## Standard Research Workflows

### Company Deep-Dive

When Chay says "tell me about [Company]" in neurotech context:

1. Technology / product summary
1. Stage (pre-clinical, clinical, commercial, public)
1. Recent funding (amount, round, date, lead investor)
1. Key indications / target market
1. Headcount estimate and hiring signals
1. Key leadership (CEO, CTO, CMO, Head of People if known)
1. Notable news (FDA clearance, trial results, partnerships)
1. Relevance to Carter Sciences (are they a recruitment target? candidate source?)

### Vertical Scan

When Chay asks "what's happening in [vertical]":

1. Top 5-8 active companies in that space
1. Recent funding or M&A activity
1. Hiring signals (job postings, LinkedIn activity, headcount growth)
1. Technology developments (FDA, clinical milestones)
1. Emerging players worth watching
1. Content angle suggestion for Substack/LinkedIn

### Hiring Signal Analysis

When Chay needs to know "who's hiring":

- Search for LinkedIn job postings in relevant companies
- Cross-reference with Hiring Map verification status
- Flag: new roles (signal of growth), multiple openings (scale-up), engineering/regulatory roles (commercial stage signal)

### Funding Tracker

Monthly: scan for neurotech funding rounds >$5M

- Source: Crunchbase, LinkedIn, company press releases, MedCity News, STAT News
- Fields to capture: Company, Amount, Round, Lead Investor, Date, Technology, Indication
- Flag if company is already in Hiring Map or should be added
